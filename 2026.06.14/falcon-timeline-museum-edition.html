SpaceX Falcon: A History of Persistence

An interactive timeline tracing how repeated failures, disciplined engineering, and relentless iteration transformed SpaceX from a startup on the verge of collapse into a pioneer of reusable spaceflight.

Engineering Through Iteration

SpaceX’s achievements were not the result of a single breakthrough. They emerged from a disciplined process of identifying, understanding, and eliminating specific failure modes. Each setback revealed new data, each redesign reduced risk, and each mission brought the company one step closer to reliable access to space and routine rocket reuse.
