👇 跑一下两个场景对比，自己看

profit, rate = 100, 0.20
A = sum(profit * (1 + rate)**y for y in range(20))
B = profit * 20
print("A · 全再投入:", round(A), "亿")
print("B · 全分掉:  ", round(B), "亿")
print("差:", round(A / B, 2), "倍")
